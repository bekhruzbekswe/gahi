What this shows

  • A button is a verb drawn for people. Your click and the agent's call change the same model.
  • The agent receives what changed and who changed it, so it doesn't need a new picture of everything.
  • pay declares its risk, so the agent can propose a payment but can't make one alone.

What could break

  • Every app has to expose this model, and most apps won't be rewritten for it.
  • An app could mislabel a risky verb, so the runtime has to enforce risk as well.
  • The token figures are estimates. Real tools trim trees and shrink screenshots, so the gap can be smaller.
