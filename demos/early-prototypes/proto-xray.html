    The app that lies

    Risk labels come from the app, and an app can get them wrong. The runtime checks what the action would do.

      What this shows

      • You can see the agent's reach on the app itself, without opening a permissions page.
      • A limit you paint changes what the agent receives right away.
      • Who wrote a field travels with the field, so a stranger's words stay data.

      What could break

      • Hiding a field can make the agent worse at its task without it knowing why.
      • Apps have to record who wrote each field, and they can get that wrong.
      • The outbox only catches effects that pass through the runtime.
      Simulated with example data. No real agent runs and no real payments are made. The agent here is scripted.
