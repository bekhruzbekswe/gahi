    What this shows

    • The agent can run at full speed because nothing on the sheet is real yet.
    • Your job shrinks from reading 203 actions to making 3 decisions.
    • Anything that can't be undone stays a separate choice and leaves only through the outbox.

    What could break

    • Most apps can't branch their data today, so drafts need support in the data layer.
    • People may still approve without reading. Sampling reduces that risk but doesn't remove it.
    • The longer a draft waits, the more conflicts it collects.
